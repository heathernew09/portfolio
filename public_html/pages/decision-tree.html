Interact with the artwork above. Navigate through the branches to uncover the path forward.

Experiential Tranquility

Part of a 1.2M sq. ft. tradeshow takeover, this experience was designed to provide a moment of tranquility and clinical clarity. Located within the "Zen Garden" booth, it served as a digital anchor for veterinary professionals seeking a break from the high-energy show floor.

Rapid AI Prototyping

Leveraging generative AI to bridge the gap between creative vision and technical execution. By using targeted prompting and iterative feedback loops, we accelerated the design process, allowing for rapid exploration of UI metaphors and botanical aesthetics.

SVG Architecture

The interactive logic is built on a hand-crafted SVG structure, optimized for fluid animation and crisp rendering at 4K resolution. Each branch and node is dynamic, responding to user input with modular JavaScript pathways that balance complexity with intuitive flow.

Record-Breaking Results

The gamification approach increased engagement substantially compared to traditional educational materials, engaging thousands of veterinary professionals during the multi-day event and setting a new benchmark for trade show interaction.
