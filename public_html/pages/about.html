Origin Story: The Hexagon

In 2005, a Design 1 challenge changed everything: create a paper container to protect an egg dropped from a flight of stairs. Inspired by soccer ball geometry, I built an inverted dome with a hexagonal structure.

It wasn't just about the drop—it was about geometric precision and thinking differently. Nearly 20 years later, that same egg is still intact, serving as a reminder that built-to-last solutions require a foundation of bold experimentation.
