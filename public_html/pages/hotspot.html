Conquesting the Floor

In a crowded tradeshow environment dominated by a massive competitor (Z*eti$), we needed a disruption strategy. While others used clinical blue and white, we pivoted to Danger Red. The goal was stopping traffic and drawing vets away from the competition.

We Set the Booth on Fire!

Balancing Visceral Drama with Clinical Accuracy

Standard educational quizzes are passive. We designed an interaction model where the user's touch literally performs the drug's function: extinguishing inflammation.

This tactile reinforcement created a stronger memory structure for the clinical data than reading alone. Veterinarians didn't just learn about Galliprant—they experienced its action.

Who won 2025 AVMA's best interactive exhibit in show?

The Sensory Stack

Immersion requires more than pixels. I orchestrated a full soundscape to cut through the tradeshow noise. The 'Crackle' drew them in; the 'Hiss' of the fire going out provided auditory satisfaction for getting the right answer.

Technical Specifications

  • Visual: 75-inch 4K Touchscreen at 3840×2160
  • Haptic/Input: Reactive Touch Hotspots
  • Audio Layers:
    • Background Crackle (Ambience)
    • Fire Swoop (Transition)
    • Steam Hiss (Success)
  • Interactive Elements: Plays video, streams audio, logo re-paint, sensory map white model

Building It: Audio

Creating an immersive experience for a tradeshow environment required both technical precision and creative problem-solving.

Audio Direction

When you're in this business you run into really talented people along the way, Dave is one of those guys. He helps out with the sounds and audio for the interactive experiences I put together. Off the shelf SFX that are good are difficult to find. The creativity Dave brings to the table is what it's all about. I can tell him what I'm envisioning and he whips it up. For this fire background he used some everyday items. The fizzles and crinkles plus the distortions help create the fire illusion.
