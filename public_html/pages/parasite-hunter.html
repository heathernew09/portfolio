Tradeshow Takeover

Set against the 'World Classic' theme of VMX 2026—the world's largest veterinary conference—we faced a sea of 700+ exhibitors and nearly 30,000 attendees at the Orange County Convention Center. In an environment defined by open-plan, high-concept experiences like Elanco's interactive Zen Garden, the Parasite Hunter AR game was designed to cut through the noise, turning the massive Expo Hall into an extension of our brand story.

The Challenge

With this years theme being "Go Beyond" we were looking to do something more than the traditional booth strategies. We needed something that would:

  • Drive qualified traffic from across the venue
  • Educate through active participation
  • Create memorable brand moments
  • Generate repeat engagement throughout the multi-day event

Meet the Characters

The Power of Four

Each active ingredient is a character. I translated complex pharmacological modes of action into distinct traits and animations—turning clinical data into the primary interactors for our gameplay loop.

Loti(laner)

Treats:

Fleas:Fast, agile jumper. Most common external parasite.

Ticks: Disease carrier. Prevents Lyme and tick-borne illnesses.

Moxi(dectin)

Treats:

Heartworm: Life-threatening internal parasite.

Pyra(ntel)

Treats:

Roundworms:Common intestinal parasite.

Hookworms: Blood-feeding parasite causing anemia.

Prazi(quantel)

Treats:

Tapeworm: The Tapeworm Segmented intestinal parasite.

The Technical Pivot

Refining the Experience

Translating a 2D comic aesthetic into a performant 3D WebAR environment required rigorous technical oversight. I acted as the Product Designer for the UX and UI, identifying solutions to friction points that would have hindered adoption in a high-traffic trade show environment.

QA & Optimization Oversight:

  • Input Sensitivity: Identified lag in hit detection during early builds; recommended Raycasting adjustments to the vendor to ensure "snappy" parasite captures.
  • Asset Performance: Optimized 3D models working with the video team and vendor to dial in the settings for performance.
  • UX Friction Audit: Providing solutions and ideas for scaling the game to actions outside of the tradeshow space. Maximizing efforts of the original game build.

The 6-Month Sprint

This activation was a "labor of love"—a rigorous development cycle that required tireless collaboration between the studio, video team, and technical vendors. My role was to ensure that throughout long days and technical hurdles, the 'Go Beyond' mission was never compromised, resulting in what leadership called Elanco’s "best VMX ever".

"I saw Heather as people gleefully killed parasites on their phone... You have all created something to be deeply proud of." — Adriana Zardus, Account Director

Engagement Strategy

Leaderboards Incite Competition

Tapeworm trophies for the winners

Great technology facilitates a story; great strategy ensures that story stays with the participant long after the event ends.

The Mini Toby Trophy served as a physical touchstone for the AR experience. By mirroring our 12-foot "Toby" installation from the previous year, we created a sense of brand continuity that rewarded repeat interaction and mastery of the game’s educational content.

Driving Sustained Interest

  • Brand Continuity: Leveraged established IP to deepen the emotional connection with the audience.
  • Tangible Value: Provided a high-quality collectible that serves as a professional "badge of honor" in a clinical setting.
  • Healthy Competition: Sparked a positive leaderboard culture, encouraging attendees to return and engage with the brand multiple times.
"We aimed for deep, meaningful engagement. By pairing an accessible AR experience with a tangible reward, we created a positive feedback loop that turned a brief digital interaction into a lasting brand memory."
